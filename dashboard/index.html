Definitions. Sales orders exclude canceled and unavailable orders. Customers are distinct people (customer_unique_id), not order-level customer ids. Amounts in BRL (Brazilian real). No profit or margin figures: the data has no costs.

Data. Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Aggregates exported from the warehouse on – UTC; every figure is tested against SQL in the repository.
